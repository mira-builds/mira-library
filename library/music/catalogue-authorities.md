# Music — catalogue authorities

Research date: 1 October 2026. Scope: the first twelve composers in the [composer roster](composer-roster.md), MUS-C001–012. This is a local source-selection record under the [Music architecture](architecture.md), not a work-level catalogue or a holdings inventory.

## Disposition

Authority routes are identified for all twelve composers. Eleven have a recommended catalogue foundation; Ravel has an identified bibliographical baseline whose preferred revision and identifier conventions still need reconciliation. Haydn requires a composite authority: retain Hoboken references but resolve attribution against the Haydn Institute's scholarship. These are curatorial selections supported by the sources below, not claims that the catalogue bodies have been fully inspected.

No full catalogue or score was saved locally, purchased, or admitted. No registry, schema, or Git publication changes were made. The access pass below advances Schubert to a readable full-text candidate and Mahler to a readable online work-list candidate; neither is locally inspected or admission-ready. The other rows retain their stated partial-access or unresolved status. Online reading and bibliographical discovery do not establish local possession or full œuvre coverage.

### Practical disposition

Begin substantive catalogue work with the readable Mahler list or Schubert's exact-edition text candidate, rather than waiting for twelve complete books. Beethoven's authentic sample is suitable for a smaller demonstration of catalogue fields. Chopin's ACO can support edition identification immediately, within its narrower scope.

For the remaining print authorities, retain exact editions and volume sequences for later library-access or acquisition work. Do not substitute a retailer's generic “digital editions” delivery notice for proof that a particular catalogue is an ebook. No scarcity claim or spending recommendation follows from this bounded search.

The Internet Archive item's uploader-supplied public-domain label was not adopted as verified rights metadata. Its readable text is a private-reading candidate; this observation does not authorize redistribution or prove scan completeness. No source text was copied into this repository.

The composer sections below retain the authority-selection evidence from the first pass. Where they say a route is uninspected, the access table above is the later, narrower update: Schubert's OCR/title matter, Beethoven's two samples, and Mahler's list have now been accessed online, but none has been admitted as a Library holding.

## MUS-C001 — Johann Sebastian Bach

**Select:** *Bach-Werke-Verzeichnis*, third expanded edition (BWV³), 2022; issued by Bach-Archiv Leipzig, prepared by Christine Blanken, Christoph Wolff, and Peter Wollny; Breitkopf & Härtel, BV 400. German. Retain Wolfgang Schmieder's earlier catalogue lineage without treating its editions as interchangeable.

**Identifiers and limits:** Record BWV identifier and catalogue edition together. The publisher says the new edition systematically incorporates work versions and reorganizes uncertain and erroneous attributions. Those distinctions belong in the work records, not in a single undifferentiated list.

**Evidence/access:** [Publisher prospectus](https://www.breitkopf.com/assets/pdf/BH_Faltblatt-DL_BWV-DE.pdf), including credits and scope, inspected as extracted text; the [publisher's 2022 announcement](https://breitkopf.podigee.io/8-monumentaler-meilenstein-das-neue-bach-werke-verzeichnis) was identified in the initial reference collection. Full volume not inspected. Next inspect its numbering conventions and use Bach digital/RISM as linked source-discovery resources, not substitutes for this edition's corpus definition.

## MUS-C002 — George Frideric Handel

**Select:** Bernd Baselt's thematic-systematic catalogue in *Händel-Handbuch*, volumes 1–3, Bärenreiter: 1978, 1984, 1986. German. The publisher lists later 2008 issues for volumes 1 and 2; an issue date alone does not establish a revised catalogue.

**Identifiers and limits:** HWV, retaining suffixes and appendix distinctions. Volume 1 covers stage works; volume 2 oratorios, vocal chamber music, and church music; volume 3 instrumental music, pasticcios, and fragments. Volume 4 is documentary material, not a fourth segment of the same work-number sequence.

**Evidence/access:** [Bärenreiter's Hallische Händel-Ausgabe and handbook listing](https://emag.baerenreiter.com/haendel-georg-friedrich/) inspected, including volumes and dates. Use the Hallische Händel-Ausgabe for score/source follow-through. Full handbook and subsequent corrections remain to inspect before fixing a coverage denominator.

## MUS-C003 — Joseph Haydn

**Select as a composite:** Anthony van Hoboken, *Joseph Haydn: Thematisch-bibliographisches Werkverzeichnis*, volumes I–III, 1957, 1971, 1978, for established numbering; Joseph Haydn Institute scholarship and the critical edition *Joseph Haydn Werke* for attribution and source corrections. German.

**Identifiers and limits:** Preserve the complete Hoboken designation, including Roman work-group numeral, number, letters, and uncertainty. Hoboken is not sufficient as the sole present-day authority: the Institute explicitly documents outdated attribution decisions, missing false attributions, and insufficient manuscript coverage.

**Evidence/access:** [Institute's catalogue assessment](https://www.haydn-institut.de/index.php/gesamtausgabe/werkverzeichnis) inspected. It describes a new online catalogue as planned; this does not establish a finished replacement. Next reconcile entries against the relevant critical-edition volume, recording disagreements rather than silently changing historical Hoboken identities. Full Hoboken volumes and work-level corrections not inspected.

## MUS-C004 — Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

**Select:** *Köchel-Verzeichnis*, 2024 revision, Breitkopf & Härtel, with the Mozarteum's KV Online as the institutional access and linking route. German print reference; online interface language is separate from source language.

**Identifiers and limits:** Store KV/K display form, catalogue edition, and earlier equivalents. The 2024 revision changes numbering policy and incorporates works absent from the earlier main sequence; do not assume a number is edition-independent.

**Evidence/access:** [Mozarteum description](https://mozarteum.at/en/digital-mozart-edition) inspected. It distinguishes functioning online core data from the developing full digital catalogue. The Neue Mozart-Ausgabe and Digital Mozart Edition provide the score route. Next inspect exact KV records and their edition concordances; online access does not establish possession of the printed catalogue or every score.

## MUS-C005 — Ludwig van Beethoven

**Select:** Kurt Dorfmüller, Norbert Gertsch, and Julia Ronge, *Ludwig van Beethoven: Thematisch-bibliographisches Werkverzeichnis*, Henle, 2014, two volumes, HN 2207, ISBN 978-3-87328-153-0. German.

**Identifiers and limits:** Preserve opus and WoO identifiers with the 2014 authority, and explicitly cross-reference older Kinsky–Halm and other catalogue references when encountered. Do not restrict the œuvre to opus numbers or assume a list of completed works accounts for sketches and unfinished projects.

**Evidence/access:** [Publisher record and contents/sample routes](https://www.henle.de/de/Dorfmueller-Gertsch-Ronge-Ludwig-van-Beethoven-Thematisch-bibliographisches-Werkverzeichnis/HN-2207) inspected; [editor's 2014 account](https://blog.henle.de/en/2014/10/27/the-new-catalogue-of-works-of-ludwig-van-beethoven/) supports publication identity. Full volumes and sample-entry bodies not inspected in this batch. Next establish the treatment of unfinished works, studies, copies, and numbering concordances from the introduction and relevant entries.

## MUS-C006 — Franz Schubert

**Select:** Otto Erich Deutsch, *Franz Schubert: Thematisches Verzeichnis seiner Werke in chronologischer Folge*, Bärenreiter, 1978, with the New Schubert Edition's work database for current editorial cross-reference. German.

**Identifiers and limits:** D numbers with letter suffixes and edition identity; opus numbers are secondary references. Preserve former numbers, fragments, alternate datings, and version relationships instead of normalizing them away.

**Evidence/access:** The [Austrian Academy's bibliography](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/kis/links/deutsch-verzeichnis-franz-schubert) identifies the 1978 volume and an external scan route; that scan was not inspected. The [Schubert database](https://schubert-ausgabe.de/recherche/schubert-datenbank/) was inspected at listing level and visibly links D numbers, chronology, opus, edition locations, and comments. Next inspect its use instructions and reconcile revisions against the 1978 baseline; a first-page inspection is not a whole-database audit.

## MUS-C007 — Frédéric Chopin

**Select:** Józef Michał Chomiński and Teresa Dalila Turło, *Katalog dzieł Fryderyka Chopina / A Catalogue of the Works of Frederick Chopin*, PWM and the Fryderyk Chopin Society, 1990, as the initial general catalogue. Retain Krystyna Kobylańska's manuscript catalogue and thematic-bibliographical catalogue as complementary source authorities. Polish/English title does not by itself prove fully parallel bilingual content.

**Identifiers and limits:** Keep opus numbers, posthumous opus assignments, and non-opus catalogue references in distinct fields. Brown and Kobylańska references encountered in editions require an explicit concordance; do not infer equivalent numbers. Manuscript and first-edition inventories answer different questions from a full work list.

**Evidence/access:** The [Society's publications record](https://tifc.chopin.pl/en/publications/) confirms the 1990 catalogue; its [work-list conventions](https://www.chopin.pl/np/alphabetic_list_of_works.en.html) explain posthumous opus handling. Its [bibliography](https://www.chopin.pl/np/pierwodruki.pl.html) identifies Brown's second edition (1972), Kobylańska's two-volume manuscript catalogue (1977), and further source research. Full catalogue bodies and exact concordance remain uninspected. Investigate the National Edition and source comparisons for score selection; do not substitute an incipit for a full score.

**Identifiers and limits:** Preserve opus, non-opus, and appendix references as printed; verify the catalogue's section conventions before assigning machine identifiers. Musical œuvre coverage is separate from Schumann's collected criticism and correspondence.

**Evidence/access:** [Publisher record](https://www.henle.de/jp/McCorkle-Robert-Schumann-Thematic-Indices/HN-2204) inspected; its interface locale does not identify the book's language. ## MUS-C009 — Johannes Brahms

**Select:** Margit L. McCorkle, *Johannes Brahms: Thematisch-Bibliographisches Werkverzeichnis*, Henle, 1984, HN 2203. German.

## MUS-C010 — Claude Debussy

**Select:** The revised 2003 catalogue accompanying François Lesure's *Claude Debussy: biographie critique suivie du catalogue de l'œuvre*. French. Retain the 1977 Lesure catalogue as a separate numbering authority.

**Identifiers and limits:** Store catalogue year with each L/Lesure-style reference; prefixes used by external sources must be preserved rather than assumed equivalent. The [existing Debussy example](debussy-catalogue-example.md) demonstrates why: *Six Épigraphes antiques* is no. 131 in the 1977 catalogue and no. 139 in 2003.

**Evidence/access:** The publisher's [complete-works account](https://www.durand-salabert-eschig.com/-/media/Images/DSE/Other-Pages/Brochures/DEB_book_08_EN-FINAL-issuu.ashx?hash=4A75A0BCC6464B75CC2BB1BFB65CF6DE1E36B0DC&la=en-GB), retrieved as search-index text, credits Anik Devriès-Lesure with the revision appended in 2003. A direct fetch of another publisher brochure failed; no full brochure-body inspection is claimed. The earlier pilot's inspected Henle preface independently supports the two catalogue numberings. Next inspect the French volume's catalogue, credits, and concordance; do not assume the English biography reproduces it unchanged. The Durand complete edition is the score-research route.

## MUS-C011 — Maurice Ravel

**Identified baseline; revision choice remains provisional:** Marcel Marnat, *Maurice Ravel*, Fayard, 1986; its embedded catalogue occupies pp. 724–784 in the BnF-described issue. French. The embedded catalogue is a distinct component of a biography.

**Identifiers and limits:** Preserve catalogue attribution and literal reference. Do not merge Marnat and Orenstein numbering on appearance alone, or use a list of principal compositions as the full œuvre denominator. Exact treatment of arrangements, lost works, and later revisions remains to inspect.

**Evidence/access:** [BnF bibliographical record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb431357224.public) inspected, explicitly locating the catalogue; [Fayard's record](https://www.fayard.fr/livre/maurice-ravel-9782213016856/) confirms the 1986 publication. BnF search discovery also surfaced a revised/corrected issue, but its exact identity and changes were not resolved. Next compare that issue's title page and catalogue with the 1986 baseline and document the numbering concordance. This row identifies the authority lineage; it does not certify a preferred latest edition.

## MUS-C012 — Gustav Mahler

**Work-map follow-through:** the [Mahler work map](mahler-work-map.md) now covers the cited GMW snapshot's 64 numbered groups, listed versions/components, and 21 unnumbered lost-work entries. This is local catalogue mapping, not score acquisition or source-body admission.

**Select:** *Gustav Mahler Werke* (GMW), International Gustav Mahler Society; catalogue framework developed in 2021–2022, introduction by Federico Celestini and Christian Utz dated March 2023. Cite the online record and access date as well as the scheme. German introduction; English work-list access also exists.

**Identifiers and limits:** GMW distinguishes versions within work groups, movements using lowercase letters, piano/orchestral song versions using K/O, and fragments/arrangements using F/B. Lost works are a separate unnumbered category. Retain those distinctions; counting numbered entries alone would omit part of its declared scope.

**Evidence/access:** [Institutional introduction](https://www.gustav-mahler.org/forschung/werkverzeichnis/einfuehrung) inspected; [work-list route](https://www.gustav-mahler.org/en/gmw) located. The introduction calls its datings provisional and contains a historical rollout notice; do not repeat that notice as proof of today's platform availability. Next inspect individual records and the [critical-edition inventory](https://www.gustav-mahler.org/en/research/complete-edition/complete-catalogue), separately verifying edition and score access.

## Application rules

For future work records, retain composer, authority title, edition/revision, exact identifier, work title, version, attribution/survival status, source locator, and verification date. An alias needs evidence of equivalence. Record conflicting scholarly judgments with their respective authorities.

Before a completeness claim, inspect the controlling catalogue's contents and conventions, enumerate its in-scope entries and exceptions, and reconcile them with the intended work records. Before a score-holding claim, inspect the actual score and record its edition and coverage. This research resolves the authority-selection task; the uninspected bodies and the Ravel revision question remain explicit downstream work.
