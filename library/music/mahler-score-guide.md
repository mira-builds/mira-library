# Mahler — score guide

Music shelf, MUS-C012. Checked 2 October 2026. Companion to the [Mahler work map](mahler-work-map.md).

This is a local bibliographic and access guide, not a score collection. No scores were purchased, downloaded into the Library, or admitted as source holdings.

## A public score sample located

Breitkopf offers an independent critical edition of Symphony 3, **PB5633**, ISMN **9790004215302**, for the final 1906 version. Its [publisher listing](https://www.breitkopf.us/products/mahler-symphony-no-3-in-d-minor-breitkopf) was inspected and links a [four-page score sample](https://www.breitkopf.com/assets/haendler/samples/9790004215302.pdf). The PDF opened in the web reader: extracted content identifies Symphony 3, the opening movement, instrumental staves, performance directions and PB5633. Screenshot rendering failed with a cache miss, so visual notation legibility was not independently checked.

This is a limited public preview, not the whole score and not the Society's 1974 UE edition. The publisher describes a fresh critical review using additional sources; that claim has not been independently tested against manuscripts. Record a distinct edition if this version is later acquired.

### Institutional alternative and practical cautions

For the Third, [Sibley Music Library's original record](https://hdl.handle.net/1802/15183) provides two score PDFs (pages 1–104 and 105–end), plus a separate colour cover. It identifies UE950 and marks the item public domain. Sibley dates publication to **1906**, whereas IMSLP describes the reprint as **circa 1901–04**; preserve both attributions until the copy is examined. The first PDF fetch returned a cache miss, and the second exceeded the web reader's size limit; the repository record and file links were read, but the score pages were not rendered.

- **Seventh:** avoid #138638 for continuous reading: IMSLP explicitly reports missing pages 137–138 and misordered pages 385–386. The selected #415756 has no such defect noted in its listing; it has not been page-audited.
- **Fifth:** plate 8951 appears across different issues. The selected first-edition scan should not be silently treated as the revised score or Kubik's critical text.
- **Tenth:** the 1924 facsimile is incomplete as a collection; the listed Adagio draft is useful historical evidence, not a complete Tenth. The preface and later performing editions have separate identities and access labels.
- For immediate reading, the Fourth offers the shortest selected complete numbered-symphony scan; Das Lied provides the direct orchestral reference for its six songs. Follow the identified historical copy while retaining the modern critical reference for editorial questions.

The historical-access search is complete at the **specific source-location** level. Full-file rendering, page-by-page completeness checks, downloads and Library admission remain unperformed.

## Related versions worth keeping separate

The Society catalogue also identifies **Titan (1893), GMW11,2**: Reinhold Kubik and Stephen Hefling, NKG 2019, UE33911, study UE36514; and **Todtenfeier, GMW30,1**: Rudolf Stephan, KGA 1988, UE13827, study UE34798. These are version-specific references, not substitutes for the final First or Second Symphony. Access was not separately checked.

For a future Library holding, retain work/version, editor, publisher, edition date, identifier, format, source/access rights, and whether a critical report is included. Historical public scans can support reading, but must retain their own edition identity rather than inherit the critical-edition label.
