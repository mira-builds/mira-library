# Gustav Mahler — work map

Local research map for Music, roster MUS-C012. Prepared 1 October 2026.

Authority: Federico Celestini and Christian Utz, [Gustav Mahler Werke (GMW)](https://www.gustav-mahler.org/en/gmw), institutional page marked updated 8 August 2025; consulted 1 October 2026. Numbering follows the [GMW introduction](https://www.gustav-mahler.org/forschung/werkverzeichnis/einfuehrung). All catalogue identifiers, titles, dates, and source-stated relationships below refer to that page. Short labels and table organization are editorial navigation aids.

## Coverage and reading key

This map covers every numbered work group and every lost-work entry on the cited page, with its listed versions and components. It is complete against that bounded catalogue snapshot, not a claim to possess Mahler's scores, all source witnesses, or every later arrangement and performing edition.

| Catalogue section | Work-group coverage | Counting rule |
|---|---:|---|
| I. Completed | 50/50 | Base numbers 1–50; K/O forms and comma-numbered versions remain subordinate |
| II. Unfinished and fragments | 8/8 | F51–F58; surviving movements do not make the parent complete |
| III. Arrangements | 6/6 | B59–B64; retain the original composer and Mahler's role |
| IV. Lost | 21/21 source entries | Local L01–L21 labels only; some entries describe multiple works |

These are 64 numbered groups plus 21 unnumbered entries, not “85 independent compositions.” GMW sometimes gives separate base numbers to related versions, notably 4/5 and 31e/32; no deduplication is silently imposed.

`GMW` is implied before identifiers in table cells unless written explicitly. K = piano form; O = orchestral form. Lowercase letters identify components; commas identify catalogue versions. Preserve literal order: `10-Ka` and `45a-K` follow different source spellings. A bare number in the first table is a grouping handle when only suffixed identifiers are printed in GMW; it does not invent a new unsuffixed catalogue record. Instrumentation is abbreviated; the source remains controlling for exact forces and text attribution.

All score holdings, score completeness, listening status, and underlying manuscript verification are **not assessed**. Catalogue coverage does not change these states. See the [authority/access map](catalogue-authorities.md), [Music architecture](architecture.md), and [critical score guide](mahler-score-guide.md) for symphonic edition identities and access evidence; the guide does not establish score holdings.

Biographical context is mapped separately in the [biography and recollection guide](mahler-biography-guide.md), with edition and access limits recorded.

## What this enables

This establishes a catalogue-level checklist for score selection and interpretation. A future score record should bind to the exact identifier/version here, name its edition and editor, and distinguish a full score, reduction, parts, fragment, and later completion. Source-linked performance or recording records can then refer to that version and its actual movement order.

Validation: fresh web-reader extracts yielded 183 distinct explicitly printed GMW entry identifiers, including versions and components; every identifier was found in the saved map. Local readback confirmed 64 distinct numbered group rows and 21 lost-entry rows. The lost list and relationship exceptions were checked against the displayed catalogue. A separate shell retrieval failed at transport authentication and supplied no source evidence; the successful web-reader extracts supplied the identifier comparison instead.

No scores, recordings, source payloads, or registry entries were acquired or admitted by building this map. All four sections are represented; detailed source criticism and the complete edition's volume-by-volume coverage remain separate work. The source's prose and full catalogue text are not reproduced as a Library source body here: this is a factual identifier/title map with selected dates and editorial guidance.
